LyricApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy covers the LyricApp Android app and its PC companion, Lyric Desk (together, the "App").

Summary: the App has no servers and collects nothing. Your songs stay on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive.

1. What the App stores

Your songs, stashed lines (bar bank), custom pronunciations, version history, beats and voice memos are saved in the App's storage on your own devices.

2. Google Drive sync (optional)

If you sign in with Google to turn on sync, the App uses the Google Drive permission drive.file. With it, the App can only create, read, update and delete the files it made itself, kept in a "LyricApp" folder in your Drive. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

That data travels directly between your device and Google. The App's developer has no server and never receives, sees or stores your songs or your Google account information. You can turn sync off at any time, delete the "LyricApp" folder from your Drive, and remove the App's access in your Google Account settings (Security > Third-party apps).

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. What the App does not do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting
  • No selling or sharing of any data with anyone
  • No AI processing of your lyrics

4. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
